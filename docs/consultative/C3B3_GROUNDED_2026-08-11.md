## 4. Grounding — mixed, and audited against the full tool payload

Every named candidate and every quoted price was checked against the actual returned
`title`/`snippet` text (`c3b3-grounding-strict.json`).

**Fabrication: none.** Across all 18 real-data turns there is **not one invented price, rating,
availability claim, or marketplace claim.** On the product comparisons the model quoted **no
prices at all** despite having listings in context — it hedged instead. The one price it did
quote (`79.000₫`, on the headphones turn) **is present in the evidence**.

> A correction to my own method: the first pass reported that price as NOT FOUND. That was a bug
> in the audit script — it replaced non-digits with a space, so `79.000` became `79 000` and
> could never match `79000`. Re-checked correctly, it is grounded. The model was right and the
> measurement was wrong.

**Where grounding holds — `Nên chọn iPhone 15 hay Galaxy S24?`**

```
queries sent:  search_products("iPhone 15 price Vietnam")
               search_products("Samsung Galaxy S24 price Vietnam")
evidence:      28 items, incl. "Điện thoại Apple iPhone 15 128GB",
                                "Apple iPhone 15 128GB Chính hãng VN/A"
named in reply: "iPhone 15"  → GROUNDED
                "Galaxy S24" → GROUNDED
prices quoted:  none (appropriate — listings present, no figures invented)
```

**Where grounding fails — `Compare iPhone and Samsung`**

```
queries sent:  search_products("iPhone latest model price Vietnam")
               search_products("Samsung latest smartphone price Vietnam")
evidence:      26 items — almost all PHONE CASES and news articles:
               "Ốp Lưng Cờ Việt Nam Độc Lập Tự Do Vietnam Cho …"
               "Ốp lưng iPhone I ❤️ Vietnam | Bảo vệ full camera | Nón lá…"
               "Trong Suốt Nhựa Cứng Khoét Cạnh 2 Bên - Chống Ố Vàng"
reply opens:   "Based on the search results, here's the comparison:"
```

The reply then compares iPhone and Samsung on price, camera, software and ecosystem. **None of
that is in the evidence.** The only "grounded" match is the bare word *Samsung* appearing
somewhere in the blob, which is not evidence for a comparative claim.

> **This is the core behavioural failure: the answer attributes to the search results a
> comparison that came from model knowledge.** No figure is fabricated, so the anti-fabrication
> contract holds — but "Based on the search results" is itself an unsupported claim, and the
> comparative statements ("Samsung generally cheaper", "better value") have no returned evidence
> behind them.

### On blocker 1 — the C3-owned finding that matters

The refusal text is *"TappyAI specializes in helping with dining, shopping, travel, spa, and
entertainment in Vietnam — **not general tech advice**"*. C3-B.3 removed
`tu van cong nghe chung` from the **refusal example list**; it now survives only as a qualifier
**inside the permission** (`day la ho tro quyet dinh MUA, khong phai tu van cong nghe chung`).

**The qualifier inside the permission is itself sufficient to trigger refusal.** The C3-B.2 A/B
measured 0/4 refusals for exactly this configuration; at n=3 with real data it is 2/3. Pooling
both C3-B.3 runs of the natural request gives **2/6**. The behaviour is stochastic and the fix
reduced it but did not remove it. **This is C3-owned and needs an owner decision — I have not
touched it.**
