# C3-B.5 — Grounding contract

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Branch:** `perf/chat-cost-phase-b` · **HEAD `59ff89a`** · nothing committed, nothing pushed

# VERDICT: C3-B.5 BLOCKED — contract implemented and locked, but grounding still fails on the harder case

The contract works on the scenario the owner cited (fabricated prices **4 → 0**) and costs nothing
in cache or LLM calls. It **does not hold** when the evidence contains no price for a named
product: the model estimates instead of saying it found none. Acceptance requires "unsupported
prices are not fabricated"; that fails.

---

## 1. Diagnosis

**The old line was not weak — it licensed the failure.** The rule being replaced read:

> `So sanh theo cac tieu chi CO DU LIEU that (gia, chat luong/danh gia, vi tri, tinh nang, ...)`

It **named "chất lượng/đánh giá, tính năng" as comparison criteria**. The model dutifully compared
on camera and features, from prior knowledge, exactly as instructed. Adding a grounding sentence
on top would have left the invitation in place — so the line is replaced, not supplemented
(mutation GR5 proves the test detects stacking).

Answers to the six questions:

1. **What the approved sentence prevented:** quoting a *figure* absent from the tool result. Only
   the numeric class.
2. **Left unprotected:** every qualitative class — camera, ecosystem, durability, resale,
   warranty network, "better overall" — plus the attribution phrasing itself.
3. **Can one clause cover both?** Yes, by naming the **evidence type** rather than enumerating
   claims: a marketplace listing proves existence, version, listed price and seller; it proves
   nothing about quality.
4. **Three states are expressible:** supported → quote it · general knowledge → mark it as the
   assistant's own view · missing → say so.
5. **No per-sentence citation needed.** The rule governs *attribution*, not citation.
6. **Usefulness preserved:** the "Ý KIẾN CHUNG" escape hatch keeps general judgement sayable, so
   the rule never degrades into "search didn't prove it → refuse".

## 2. The contract as implemented

Three sentences replacing one, in the comparison `stageBlock` (`dynamic`), placed **after
`DANH DOI` and immediately before `NGHIENG VE`**:

* **NGUON CUA TUNG CAU** — a tool result is a listing/article. It proves product/version, listed
  price, seller, and rating *if the field is actually present*. It does **not** prove camera
  quality, performance, durability, ecosystem, resale value, or "tốt hơn nói chung".
* **CHI dan gia/thong so/danh gia CO trong ket qua tool** — a side with no data must be stated as
  *chưa tìm thấy giá*; no estimating, no inferring from prior knowledge.
* **Y KIEN CHUNG** — a general judgement may still be given, but must be marked as the
  assistant's own view and **never** attributed to the search. Both phrasings are banned by name:
  `"theo ket qua tim kiem"` and `"based on the search results"` — the model answers in the user's
  language, so a Vietnamese-only ban would have left the exact English phrasing measured in
  C3-B.4 unguarded.

Plus one targeted carve-out on `NGHIENG VE`: *leaning on the user's stated priorities is not a
data claim — the source rule does not forbid concluding.*

## 3. The pick trade-off (STEP 3)

C3-B.4 measured picks falling 3/3 → 1/3 when the bare grounding sentence was added. Diagnosis:
**avoidable side effect, not necessary caution** — the model was treating a priority-based lean as
if it needed evidence. The carve-out addresses exactly that.

| | pick |
|---|---|
| no grounding rule (C3-B.4 variant A) | 3/3 |
| bare grounding sentence (C3-B.4 variant B) | 1/3 |
| **this contract, with the carve-out** | **2/3 on S1** (3/9 overall) |

Partially restored, small n. The grounding guarantee was not weakened to buy it back.

### The precise remaining defect

> **The contract's positive branch is obeyed; its negative branch never fires.**
> "Quote only what is in the result" works when the result *has* the price (S1).
> "Say you did not find a price for that side" fired **0 of 9 times** — in both measurement rounds.
> When the evidence lacks a price for a named product, the model estimates rather than admitting
> the gap.

False attribution: **3/9** (down from 4/6 in C3-B.4, still non-zero). Opinion-flagging fired 2/9.
