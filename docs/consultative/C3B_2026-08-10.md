## 4. Why this is BLOCKED

### 4.1 The approved objective fails in Vietnamese

Scenario 14-vi, "Nên mua iPhone hay Samsung?" — the exact case the owner approved — **still
refuses**, in both runs:

> "…**TappyAI specializes in helping you with dining, shopping, travel, spa & beauty, and
> entertainment in Vietnam** — not general tech advice."

The scope refactor did not unblock it. In the after-run the refusal was also delivered in
English for a Vietnamese question; with n = 1 that language flip cannot be separated from
sampling variance, but the refusal itself reproduces across both runs.

Likely cause, stated as a hypothesis and **not verified**: within `scopeBlock` the permission
is one sentence while the prohibition is two sentences plus `TUYET DOI KHONG` under a
`LUAT CUNG KHONG DUOC VI PHAM` header. The prohibition outweighs the permission. Confirming
this requires an isolated A/B on the block, which is out of C3-B's approved scope.

### 4.2 In English it became a form, not a comparison

14-en did stop refusing (a real directional gain over baseline) but replaced the refusal with
**8 clarification questions** — budget, camera, screen, OS, battery, features, which models —
against a documented ceiling of one. Baseline asked 1.

### 4.3 Comparison is ungrounded

All six comparison scenarios fired the comparison stage — verified directly against
`detectDecisionStage`, all six return `'comparison'` — yet **`search_products` was never
called** (`tools=0` on 05-vi/en, 09-vi, 14-vi/en). The approved scope is "grounded in
`search_products` results". The prompt now demands grounding; the model does not fetch it.
Prompt text alone did not induce the tool call.

### 4.4 Option narrowing failed in English

`2-4` did not narrow anything. English food scenarios went 6→11, 5→11, 3→9 bold spans.
(Bold spans count prices and dish names too, so this is a weak proxy — but it moved the
wrong way, not merely a noisy sideways.)

### 4.5 Trade-off and calibrated pick did not arrive

Trade-off fell 9→6. Pick moved 1→2 out of 40 — across the six comparison scenarios where
`NGHIENG VE` is explicitly instructed, **pick fired 0/6 in both runs**.

## 5. Not caused by C3-B

12-vi and 20-vi produce byte-identical English replies to Vietnamese prompts in both runs —
pre-existing, and already scoped to C5.

## 6. Recommendation

Do not merge C3-B on the strength of its green tests. The instruction set is now correct and
guarded, which is a prerequisite, but four of the five behavioural targets are unmet and one
regressed. The evidence points at a structural cause rather than wording — an instruction
that competes with a stronger prohibition, and a comparison path that never fetches data —
which is a different fix from the one C3-B was scoped to make.

Awaiting owner review.
