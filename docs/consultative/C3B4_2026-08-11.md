## 1. What was proven

### Failure B is real, reproducible, and worse than C3-B.3 reported

The scenario C3-B.3 held up as the **good** one — `Nên chọn iPhone 15 hay Galaxy S24?` — fabricates
a price in **5 of 5** runs:

| claim | verdict |
|---|---|
| `iPhone 15 … around **22.99 million VND**` | **GROUNDED** — evidence contains `22.990.000đ` |
| `Galaxy S24 … typically **18–20 million VND**` | **⚠ NOT IN EVIDENCE** |
| `S24 is cheaper by ~**3–5 million VND**` | **⚠ NOT IN EVIDENCE** (derived from the above) |

…all under the heading **"Based on current pricing in Vietnam"**.

**The evidence does not merely fail to support the Samsung figure — it contradicts it.** Of 28
returned items, 13 mention Samsung/Galaxy, and the only device prices among them are
`11,990,000₫` (S24 FE) and `1,199,000₫`. The reply's 18–20 million is roughly **8 million VND
above** anything returned.

**Attribution is near-universal:** 13 of 13 runs that fetched used a phrase asserting the answer
came from search — `"Based on the search results"`, `"Based on current pricing in Vietnam"`,
`"Based on current market data"`. C3-B.3 under-counted this because its detector only matched
the first phrasing.

**Qualitative claims are structurally ungroundable.** Replies assert camera quality, ecosystem
lock-in, software longevity, resale value, AI features and *"Samsung has more service centers in
Vietnam"*. The evidence is marketplace listings and sales-volume news. A Shopee listing can
support existence, variant and price — **never** "iPhone edges out in video". This is not
fixable by better retrieval; it is a claim-type problem.

### The single-sentence fix works — validated A/B

Variant B added **one sentence** to the comparison `stageBlock` (which lives in `dynamic`, so the
B1 cached `shared` prefix is untouched by construction), instructing the model to quote only
figures present in the tool result, to say plainly when one side has no price found, and not to
imply an estimate came from search.

| | money claims not in evidence | trade-off | pick | clarifications | output tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (current) | **3** | 3/3 | 3/3 | 1 | 548–575 |
| **B** (+1 sentence) | **0** | 3/3 | **1/3** | 1 | 461–521 |

Fabrication went **3 → 0** across 6 runs while the comparison stayed useful: still fetches, still
one clarification (no questionnaire), trade-offs intact, ~10% fewer output tokens.
`promptBuilder.ts` was restored and hash-verified identical.

**Honest cost of the fix:** calibrated picks dropped **3/3 → 1/3**. Being stricter about evidence
makes the model less willing to commit. That is a real behavioural trade-off and an owner
decision, not mine to ship silently.

### Confirmed unchanged

* `Should I buy an iPhone or a Samsung?` — **8 questions, 0 tool calls, 5/5**, byte-stable.
* `So sánh iPhone với Samsung` — **3 questions, 0 tool calls, 5/5**. Same shape, second instance.
* Both are the known separate questionnaire issue. Not touched.

## 2. What was disproven

### Failure A did not reproduce — 0 refusals in 25 runs

| probe | refusals |
|---|---|
| `Nên mua iPhone hay Samsung?` | **0/5** |
| `Should I buy an iPhone or a Samsung?` | 0/5 |
| `Nên chọn iPhone 15 hay Galaxy S24?` | 0/5 |
| `Compare iPhone and Samsung` | 0/5 |
| `So sánh iPhone với Samsung` | 0/5 |

Pooled across every C3-B.3 and C3-B.4 run of the natural request: **2 refusals in 11 runs ≈ 18%**
(0/3 fallback · 2/3 real · 0/5 here).

**⛔ STOP CONDITION: causality cannot be established at any affordable sample size.** Detecting
whether a prompt variant moves an ~18% event needs roughly 60+ runs *per variant* for usable
power. Three variants at that depth is well over a thousand model turns. Running the requested
n≈5 A/B would have produced a number with no power behind it — the same mistake that made the
original C3-B verdict unreliable. **I did not run an underpowered A/B and I am not claiming the
remaining qualifier is or isn't the cause.**

What *is* established: the refusal is **not** driven by C5 language contamination.
`Nên chọn iPhone 15 hay Galaxy S24?` resolves to `en` exactly like `Nên mua iPhone hay Samsung?`
and refused 0/5 while grounding 5/5. The pattern tracks **brand-level vagueness**, not language.

### Also disproven: "evidence is mostly phone cases" is not stable

C3-B.3 characterised `Compare iPhone and Samsung` as retrieving mostly accessories. Re-measured:
26 items, **16 device-relevant**, 3 accessories. Serper results vary per run. The grounding
failure is **not** caused by bad retrieval — it happens with good evidence too, because the
claims are of a type listings cannot support.

## 8. Verdict

# C3-B.4 BLOCKED

Acceptance requires **both** failures resolved. Failure A hit an explicit stop condition —
diagnosis cannot establish causality — and Failure B's fix, while validated, carries a
behavioural trade-off (picks 3/3 → 1/3) that is the owner's call.

**Three decisions are needed:**

1. **Ship the grounding-honesty sentence alone?** It removes fabricated prices (3→0) and costs
   some pick confidence. It is one sentence in `dynamic`, no cache impact, no new LLM call.
2. **How to handle Failure A?** Either accept ~18% as tolerable and close it, or authorise a
   high-power run (~60 turns/variant, roughly 1,500+ Serper credits) to establish causality.
3. **Qualitative claims (blocker 3).** Nothing in the current contract stops the model asserting
   camera or ecosystem quality from marketplace listings. That needs a scope decision, not a
   wording tweak.

Nothing committed, nothing pushed, no production file modified. Dev server stopped.
