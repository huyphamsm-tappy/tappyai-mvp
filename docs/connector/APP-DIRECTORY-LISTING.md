## Full description

> **Tappy is an AI service built around Vietnam**: its destinations, its businesses, how people here eat, shop and travel, and the questions they actually ask. Its aim is not more information but a clearer decision.
>
> **Through the connector**, an AI assistant can ask Tappy for:
> - places, restaurants and attractions in Vietnam, with address, district, rating, price level and opening status;
> - hotel rates per night, requested from Agoda, with Agoda's tracked booking link;
> - product search with listed prices from Vietnamese shops;
> - a draft itinerary built from those results.
>
> **In the Tappy app** (not part of the connector), Tappy also offers newspaper-style answers with tables and photos; whole-trip plans that include coach and train options from Vexere, flight search links and local dishes to try; deep research with cited reports; shopping offers with Lazada; social clips and a messenger inbox; and Scam Shield, which uses the scam warnings published on the public website of Vietnam's Ministry of Public Security (bocongan.gov.vn), reviewed by Tappy every week. Scam Shield is not connected to the Ministry's databases and does not verify anything directly with them; a message that matches no published warning is not proven safe.
>
> Prices and availability are indicative; the partner's page is the source of truth. Tappy does not book, pay or purchase on your behalf.
